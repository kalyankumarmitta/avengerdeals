About KDLS Deals

KDLS Deals is a deals-tracking site focused on home and kitchen gadgets sold on Amazon India. The goal is simple: spend less time hunting for a good price and more time using things that actually work in your home.

What we cover

We look at products across kitchen appliances, cookware, home essentials, and tools — items that are useful for everyday life in an Indian household. We do not cover every category on Amazon; we keep the scope narrow so the picks stay relevant.

How products are chosen

A deal is shared when the price drops meaningfully compared to recent history, the product has a reasonable number of genuine reviews, and it fits within the categories this site covers. We do not recommend products based on how much commission they earn, and we do not manufacture opinions about how well a product performs.

Who this is for

Anyone shopping on Amazon India who wants to catch a good deal without checking the site every day. If you join the Telegram channel, you get notified when something worth buying goes on sale.
